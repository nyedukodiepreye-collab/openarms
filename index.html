Questions people ask

Is the training really free?

Yes. Registering and joining the training costs nothing.

Do I need any experience?

No. Every skill starts from the basics, so beginners are welcome.

Can I choose more than one skill?

Choose the skill you most want to learn when you register. Our team will advise you on other options.

How long does each programme take?

Duration depends on the skill. Our team will confirm the schedule when they contact you.

Who is Heroes of Faith?

Heroes of Faith is our partner in delivering this training and supporting learners.
